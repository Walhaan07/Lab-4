Riverside Community Library

An ordinary, well behaved page. Press the extension button and the scan should report no warnings, or only very minor ones.

Opening hours

The library is open Monday to Friday from 9am to 6pm, and on Saturday from 10am to 4pm. Members can renew loans at the desk or through the self service machines in the lobby.

Our reading room seats forty people and has free wireless access. The local history collection is kept on the second floor and may be consulted on request.

Events this month

  • Tuesday reading group — short stories by regional authors.
  • Thursday coding club for children aged 9 to 14.
  • Saturday morning story time in the children’s corner.

Contact us

Write to desk@riverside-library.example or call the front desk during opening hours. Our privacy notice explains what we store about borrowers and for how long.
